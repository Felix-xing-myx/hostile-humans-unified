# Mod Listing Description (English)

## About

Derived from [Hostile Humans](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/hostile-humans), Hostile Humans Unified incorporates content from **Human Gunner** and adds original gameplay. It is a standalone Forge mod and does not require either original mod JAR.

## Features

The mod adds Roamers and Tier I, II, and III humans with distinct attributes, equipment, and combat capabilities. Identity badges and faction relationships affect how humans respond to players. Contracts let players hire soldiers, while a radio provides access to their roster and orders.

Hired soldiers can follow, guard an area, hold a position, or patrol. They can be set to Active Attack, Passive Protection, or Fully Neutral. Players can open a soldier's inventory to manage equipment, configure item pickup, or dismiss them.

Humans use melee weapons, shields, bows, crossbows, tridents, or firearms according to their equipment. At low health, they retreat and try to recover. Signal flares summon temporary allies or hostile humans, and the radio can show soldier status and recall them.

## Optional Integrations

TaCZ adds firearm support, Better Combat provides its melee combat integration, and Curios adds a dedicated identity badge slot. Spartan Weaponry, Spartan Shields, and Immersive Armors are detected automatically for compatible weapons, shields, and armor. These mods are optional and are not required to run Hostile Humans Unified.

Requires Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge. See the [configuration guide](../CONFIGURATION.md) for details.
